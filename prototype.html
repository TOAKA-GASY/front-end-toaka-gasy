Behind the legend

A tradition born
in the valleys of Betsileo

"A symbol of unity and hospitality — poured to welcome a guest, to bless a marriage, to bring people together at the moments that matter most."

Across Madagascar, rum has always been part of every tradition. It's called toaka gasy — often made in far places where wild nature gives it its finest taste. The one that inspired us most comes from the south, in the hidden valleys of the Betsileo highlands.

So we crafted a bottle to honour it — to show the world how rum can be enjoyed: as a spirit of togetherness and a drink of celebration.

Meet the founders
The founders

Two worlds, one bottle

Joscha (Narindra)
Co-founder · Madagascar

I grew up surrounded by bottles and customers. My mother started a wholesale spirits business in Antananarivo in 1989 — and watching her build it showed me what dedication really looks like. With Toaka Gasy, my goal is to bring a piece of Madagascar to the world. Every bottle reflects where I come from, the values I grew up with, and the passion passed down through my family.

Gerben
Co-founder · Netherlands

Dutch by birth, shaped by festivals and movement. For over fifteen years I worked behind the scenes of large celebrations — on the technical side of beverages. Over time, I realised it was never just about the drinks. It was about connection. That's where Toaka Gasy found me — not as a project, but as a purpose. A bridge between two worlds.
